Privacy Policy

1. Who is responsible

Join is a student project of the Developer Akademie. Responsible for data processing are the students listed in the Legal Notice: [Student Names List], [Address], Email: [Email].

2. Which data we store

When you use Join, we store the following data:

  • Account data: name, email address and password (only if you sign up)
  • Contacts you create: name, email address and phone number
  • Tasks you create: title, description, due date, priority and subtasks

Please do not enter real personal data of other people. Join is a demo project and should only be used with test data.

3. Why we store it

We only use your data to provide the functions of Join: logging in, showing your board and managing tasks and contacts. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Where the data is stored

The data is stored in a Firebase Realtime Database, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. More information can be found in the Firebase privacy information.

5. Local storage

Join saves your theme setting (light or dark mode) in the local storage of your browser. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data. You can also object to the processing or ask us to restrict it. Just contact us at [Email]. You also have the right to file a complaint with a data protection authority.

7. How long we keep the data

We keep your data as long as the project is online. Test data may be reset or deleted at any time without notice.
